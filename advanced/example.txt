Barnaby is a ginger tabby of substantial "heft" and even greater personality. He doesn’t merely live in the house; he grants his humans the privilege of maintaining it for him. With fur the color of a slightly burnt piece of toast and eyes like polished jade, he is the undisputed master of his domain.
Barnaby’s charm lies in his selective affection. He isn't a lap cat by default; you have to earn it through a complex series of chin scritches and the silent acknowledgement that he is, in fact, the boss. When he finally does settle on your knees, vibrating with a deep, rhythmic thrum, it feels less like a cuddle and more like a royal blessing.
